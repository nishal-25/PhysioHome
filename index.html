PhysioHome

Heal at home, Guided every step.

Follow your doctor's exercise plan from your own living room. PhysioHome shows you each move in 3D, watches your form through your camera, and shares your progress with your doctor, so recovery doesn't need a long trip to the clinic.

No routine trips to the clinic Works with a basic webcam Progress is saved on the platform Regular reports are checked by your doctor

See it before you do it

A rotating 3D figure shows each exercise, at normal or slow speed.

Corrected as you move

Skeletal lines follow your body and turn green when you reach the target angle.

Your doctor stays in the loop

Accuracy, reps and pain scores arrive as reports the doctor can review any time.

How it works

  1. Register with your health details and choose your doctor.
  2. The doctor assigns exercises, reps and instructions.
  3. You exercise beside the 3D demo while the app tracks you.
  4. The doctor reviews progress and adjusts the plan.
